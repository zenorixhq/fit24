2. How 1-on-1 PT Elevation Works (Floor to Personal Training)

A common question from management is: "How does a member become 1-on-1 PT enabled? Is it when we add PT points?"

YES — PT SESSIONS & COACH ASSIGNMENT AUTOMATICALLY TRIGGER ELEVATION

An athlete is considered 1-on-1 PT Enabled when they have an assigned coach and active PT session credits (ptSessionsTotal > 0). As soon as session credits are purchased or allotted, the platform elevates the athlete across all portals instantly with zero manual reconfiguration.
