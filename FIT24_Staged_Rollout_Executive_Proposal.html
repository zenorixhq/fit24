Executive Philosophy: Why Staged Rollout Guarantees Opening-Day Success

ZERO OPENING-DAY CHAOS

Most luxury gym openings fail due to software overload on Day 1: forcing 200 excited new members to download apps, configure logins, and queue at the desk while trainers struggle with new interfaces. FIT24 avoids this completely with a phased launch: Month 1 concentrates 100% on the Front Desk CRM to collect membership fees, track floor check-ins & check-outs, issue 18% GST invoices, and lock sauna/ice bath access. Once reception runs like clockwork, Month 2 equips trainers, and Month 3 presents the member mobile portal as an exclusive VIP upgrade.
