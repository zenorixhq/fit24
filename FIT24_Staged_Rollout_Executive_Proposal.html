1. Stage 1: Month 1 — Front Desk & Club Operational CRM Mastery (HEAVY FOCUS)

During Month 1, only the front desk computers and management consoles run the system. No member or trainer needs to download or log in to anything. All control, billing, attendance, and facility quotas remain securely managed at the front desk.

⚡ 1. 3-Second Fast Check-In & Headcount
FRONT DESK
Optical camera scanner for member QR passes + instant search bar. Desk staff types 2 letters of a member's name, phone, or code (e.g. F24-1001) and taps check-in in under 3 seconds.
Business Impact: Eliminates reception bottlenecks during peak morning (6–9 AM) and evening (5–9 PM) rushes. Live counter displays exact real-time headcount inside the facility.
📋 2. Comprehensive Member CRM Directory
DATABASE
Centralized athlete register storing contact details, blood group, emergency contacts, registration date, duration (1, 3, 6, 12 months), and plan tier (Elite, Prime, Pro). Searchable with 1 click.
Business Impact: Replaces fragile paper notebooks and messy Excel sheets with a secure, centralized cloud database accessible only by authorized staff.
🥊 3. Desk-Controlled PT Quota & Coach Tracker
PT CONTROL
Tracks assigned personal trainer (Coach Rahul Menon, Coach Anjali Nair, Coach Vikram Das) and allocated PT session credits (e.g. 6, 12, or 24 sessions) directly from the member CRM table.
Business Impact: Prevents disputes between clients and trainers. When an athlete buys PT, desk credits sessions; when a session is conducted, desk debits -1 PT.
🧖 4. Strict Facility Quota Passbook
ZERO LEAKS
Enforces tier quotas for Infrared Sauna (e.g. 4/4) and Cold Plunge Ice Baths. Desk taps Deduct Quota to log entry, updating remaining balances instantly with zero guesswork.
Business Impact: Stops unauthorized usage. In unmanaged gyms, non-entitled members sneak into saunas/ice baths, costing thousands in electricity and ice maintenance.
☕ 5. Member Café POS & Auto-Discount Wallet
REVENUE BOOST
Prepaid member wallet for protein shakes, smoothies, and snacks. Automatically applies tier-based discounts (10% for Prime, 15% for Elite) and deducts bill amount from prepaid balance.
Business Impact: Encourages members to prepay ₹1,000–₹5,000 into their gym wallet upfront, locking in café revenue and speeding up shake pickups post-workout.
🎱 6. Snooker Lounge & Guest Pass Allocator
AMENITIES
Tracks complimentary guest passes (e.g. 2 passes for Elite members) and snooker lounge hours. Logs guest name and member sponsor to preserve club exclusivity.
Business Impact: Turns guest passes into a high-converting sales funnel. Guests who visit with an Elite member are automatically captured as warm leads.
⏸ 7. Official Brochure-Compliant Pause Engine
POLICY CONTROL
Enforces FIT24's official policy: freeze/pause is strictly valid only on plans $\ge 3$ months, for a minimum of 7 consecutive days (max 2 blocks). Automatically recalculates expiry date.
Business Impact: Eliminates staff favoritism and unauthorized membership extensions while giving legitimate traveling athletes clear, fair pause terms.
🧾 8. 18% GST Tax Receipt & Daily Cashbook
ACCOUNTING
Generates compliant GST tax invoices (9% CGST + 9% SGST). Live daily financial closing ledger categorizes every rupee into UPI / GPay, Cash, and Card/POS before desk sign-off.
Business Impact: Simplifies monthly GST filing for your CA. Zero cash discrepancies between receptionist shift handovers.
⚠️ 9. 14-Day Automated Churn & Expiry Shield
RETENTION
Proactive radar that automatically filters all members whose plans expire within the next 7 to 14 days. Provides a 1-click WhatsApp renewal reminder pre-filled with their details.
Business Impact: Prevents members from continuing to train unpaid while lifting renewal conversion rates by 25% through timely, courteous reminders.
🎯 10. VIP Lead Capture & Inquiry CRM Pipeline
SALES PIPELINE
Captures website VIP tour requests, phone inquiries, and walk-in prospects. Tracks stages (New Inquiry → Tour Booked → Trial Active → Converted Member).
Business Impact: Ensures no prospective high-paying lead falls through the cracks during the Grand Opening marketing campaign.

3. Stage 2: Month 2 — Trainer Command Hub & Coaching Operations

Once desk staff operates the CRM with 100% confidence, personal trainers receive mobile access to their dedicated coaching portal. Trainers operate in an isolated environment—they see only their assigned clients and floor athletes, with zero access to club revenue or desk settings.

🥊 Dedicated 1-on-1 Client Roster
ISOLATED VIEW
Trainers see their assigned client list, remaining PT sessions, and automated Adherence Score (0–100%) calculated from attendance and weekly check-in completion.
⚡ 7-Day Day-by-Day Split Builder
PROGRAMMING
Coaches prescribe custom workouts from a dropdown of 72 movements across 10 body categories, setting target weights, tempo cues (e.g. 3-0-1-0), and biomechanical coaching notes.
🎟 1-Click Floor Session Debit (-1 PT Done)
ACCOUNTABILITY
After training a client on the floor, the coach taps -1 PT Done directly on their phone. This logs the session in the unified audit log and updates remaining session counts.
💬 Sunday Reflection Review Queue
CLIENT RETENTION
Dedicated inbox where trainers review weekly client check-ins (energy rating, obstacles, wins), reply with debrief notes, and adjust the upcoming week's training split.
⚡ Floor Athlete Claiming (Sales Funnel)
PT UPGRADES
Coaches can view unassigned gym floor athletes and tap ⚡ Claim to Me upon PT package purchase, instantly moving the member into their active 1-on-1 roster.
🩺 PAR-Q Health Screening & Risk Flags
SAFETY & LIABILITY
Pre-exercise Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire flags cardiac, orthopedic, or blood pressure contraindications (⚠️ Caution) to protect club liability.
